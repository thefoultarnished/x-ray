v2.1Latest Oct 16, 2026

Refresh a single result, see accounts with no public location, and a smarter Auto reveal.

New
Refresh a resultSaved results can go out of date. Click the refresh icon on a pill to look that account up again and update its saved result. If X does not answer, the old result stays.
Hidden locationsWhen an account's location isn't known or public, you now get a coral Hidden pill with a lock instead of Not available. It is saved like any result and is not counted as a failed lookup.
Improved
Smarter Auto revealJump to a new part of the feed and the old lookups are cancelled, then the top post on screen is looked up first after a short pause. It also looks up two posts above and below, instead of one.
Neater globeRegions show a small globe in a flag-sized chip, so it lines up with the flags.
v2.0 Oct 16, 2026

A toolbar popup with Auto reveal, and many fixes for privacy, safety and stuck results. Needs Chrome, Edge or Brave 130 or newer.

New
Toolbar popupClick the X-Ray icon to turn on Auto reveal, choose the cooldown between lookups, clear cached records or support the project.
Auto revealTurn it on in the popup and X-Ray looks up the accounts on your screen by itself, one at a time, plus the post just above and below. You pick the cooldown between lookups (2, 3, 5 or 10 seconds). It is off by default and pauses for 5 minutes if lookups keep failing. X may rate limit you if you use it a lot.
Clear cached recordsSee how many saved locations you have and clear them from the popup. Click Clear twice to confirm.
Improved
Simpler result pillClicking a result no longer copies it. Hover it for the details instead.
New logoX-Ray has a fresh icon: a blue bird with a location pin on a dark rounded square.
Side info cardThe details popup now opens to the right of the pill instead of covering the text above or below, and automatically moves to the left if there is not enough room.
Fixed
Popups close when you leave the tabThe details popup and the pin hint no longer stay open after you hover them, switch to another tab or window, and come back.
No more stuck "Locating..."If X doesn't answer within 10 seconds, the pill shows "Not available" for a moment and then turns back into a pin you can click to try again.
Safer resultsLocations and app names from X are always shown as plain text, so they can never add buttons, links or anything else to the page.
No more fake resultsX-Ray now only accepts a location from its own lookup, for the account you clicked, so other scripts on X can't show fake locations or interrupt a lookup.
X keeps its own protectionsX-Ray used to switch off X's safety settings on every X page shown inside a frame. Now it only allows the one profile page it looks up to load in its hidden frame, and leaves everything else as X intended.
Saved results now expireLocations you looked up are kept for 90 days from when you looked them up, up to 10,000, instead of piling up forever.
Your lookups stay privateSaved results now live in X-Ray's own storage, so X can no longer see which accounts you looked up. Results you already had are moved over automatically.
The right flag for every countryFlags now match the full country name, so Belarus no longer shows the US flag and Canada, Mexico and the Caribbean get their own flags. Over 250 countries and territories are covered, and accounts based in Europe get the EU flag.
Works with the keyboardYou can Tab to the pin and result pills, see where focus is, and screen readers now read out each button's name and the lookup result.
Flags stay privateCountry flags are now built into X-Ray instead of loaded from another website, so nobody else can see your IP address or which countries you look up.
Several other bug fixesSmaller fixes to keyboard use, flags and names, hints and cards, lookups and speed.
